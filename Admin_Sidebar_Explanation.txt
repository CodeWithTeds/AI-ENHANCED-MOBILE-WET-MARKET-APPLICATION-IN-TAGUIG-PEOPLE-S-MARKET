TAGUIG SUKI - ADMIN SIDEBAR EXPLANATION
=====================================

1. DASHBOARD
The Dashboard is your main overview and control center. Think of it as the front page where you can see everything happening in the system at a glance. It shows you important numbers like total users, total vendors, total sales, pending approvals, low stock alerts, and recent orders. You don't need to click around to know what's going on, the dashboard summarizes it all for you in one place.

2. USER MANAGEMENT
User Management is where you handle all the regular users or customers, which are the buyers on the platform. Here you can view their accounts, check their personal details, and activate or deactivate their accounts if needed. If a user has an issue, violates a rule, or needs support, this is where you manage them. It gives you full control over who can access and use the system as a buyer.

3. VENDOR MANAGEMENT
Vendor Management is the main section for everything related to sellers. This is the parent menu that groups all vendor-related functions together. It covers how vendors register, how their information is handled, and how they operate inside the marketplace. It's basically your main hub for controlling the seller side of the business.

4. ALL VENDORS
All Vendors is the complete list of every vendor who has registered in the system, whether they are already approved, still pending, or already active. You can view each vendor's profile, see their store information, and monitor their status and performance. This page helps you keep track of all sellers in one organized list.

5. STALL MANAGEMENT
Stall Management is for handling the stalls in the market, whether they are physical stalls in the palengke or virtual stalls online. Here you can assign which vendor owns which stall, see the stall number and location, and check if a stall is occupied or still vacant. It helps you organize the marketplace layout properly and avoid double assignments.

6. PENDING APPROVAL
Pending Approval is like your to-do list for approvals. All new vendor registrations, new stall requests, or product listings that need to be reviewed will appear here. You will need to check their details and documents carefully, then decide whether to Approve or Reject them. Nothing goes live on the platform until you approve it here.

7. DOCUMENTS
Documents is where all the required papers from vendors are stored and verified. This includes business permits, valid IDs, DTI certificates, barangay clearances, and other requirements. You can view each file, check if it is complete, valid, or expired, and confirm if the vendor is legitimate. This section is very important for security and compliance.

8. PRODUCT MANAGEMENT
Product Management is where you oversee all the products being sold by all vendors in the system. You can see what items are listed, check their descriptions, and categories, and remove or edit any prohibited or incorrect products. This ensures that only proper and allowed products are visible to customers.

9. PAYMENT AND ORDER MANAGEMENT
Payment and Order Management combines order monitoring and payment tracking into a single unified dashboard section. Here you can monitor order statuses (pending, in progress, completed, cancelled) and payment methods (GCash, Cash, Maya) using visual distribution graphs, without exposing individual customer names or transaction amounts.

12. AI RECOMMENDATIONS
AI Recommendations is the smart and automated feature of the system. The AI studies sales data, product trends, and customer buying behavior to give useful suggestions. For example, it can suggest what products are trending, what a customer might want to buy next, or what a vendor should restock. It helps both admins and vendors make smarter business decisions.

13. REPORTS & ANALYTICS
Reports & Analytics is where all the data is turned into useful insights and reports. You can view sales reports, best-selling products, vendor performance, and sales charts by day, week, or month. This is very helpful for monitoring the growth of the marketplace and for presenting clear reports to the admin or the LGU.

14. FEEDBACK & COMPLAINTS
Feedback & Complaints is where you can see all the reviews, ratings, feedback, and complaints sent by customers. It includes comments about vendors, products, and order experiences. This section helps you resolve issues quickly, warn or guide vendors if needed, and maintain trust and good service quality in the marketplace.
